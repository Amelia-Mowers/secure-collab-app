Community

Questions, feedback, and release notes — in a public Matrix room.

#community:tidework.io
Join the room Open in Element

You do not need a TideWork account. The room is on the Matrix network, so any Matrix account works — one you already have, or a free one from matrix.org. The first link lets you pick your own client; the second opens Element in your browser with nothing to install.

This room is public and unencrypted

Worth being plain about, given what TideWork is. Your workspaces are end-to-end encrypted and we cannot read them. This room is the opposite by design: it is world-readable, anyone can read the history without joining, and anyone who joins next year can read what you write today. Assume anything you post here is public, because it is.

So: no recovery keys, no invite links to private workspaces, and nothing you would not put on a forum. If you need to send us something sensitive, email tideworksupport@proton.me instead.

What it is for

  • Questions about using TideWork, and answers that other people can find later.
  • Feature requests and feedback — including the unflattering kind.
  • Release notes and anything going on with the hosted service.
  • Bug reports, though anything with a security dimension should go to tideworksupport@proton.me first — see the security policy.

Be decent to each other. Spam, harassment and off-topic recruiting get removed, and so does whoever brought them. That is the whole moderation policy until it needs to be longer.

Service status is elsewhere

If TideWork is down, this room runs on the same homeserver and may be down with it. tidework.io/status is the place to check, and it is a static page on separate infrastructure.
