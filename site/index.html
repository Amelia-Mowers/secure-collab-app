TideWork

Your team's workspace, actually encrypted.

TideWork is an open-source collaborative workspace — tables, kanban boards, and documents that sync in real time — built on the Matrix protocol with genuine end-to-end encryption. Your server never sees your data. Neither do we.

Open TideWork Source on GitHub

🔐 End-to-end encrypted

Every cell, card, and document is encrypted on your device with Matrix's Megolm protocol. Auditable in the open-source code — not marketing.

🌐 Federated & sovereign

Use our hosted server or point the app at your own Matrix homeserver. Collaborate across servers. Your identity and data stay yours.

📊 Everything is a table

One data model under tables, kanban, and document views — with conflict-free real-time sync across all your devices.
